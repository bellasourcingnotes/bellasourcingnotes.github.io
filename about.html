About Bella

I help overseas teams make China-side work more visible and executable.

Based in Shenzhen, I bring more than ten years of sourcing and project experience to the part of the process that is hardest to manage from abroad: local communication, detail follow-up and practical verification.

China sourcing & project execution

More than finding suppliers.

Supplier names are easy to find. The difficult work begins after that: understanding whether a supplier can actually handle the requirement, translating technical or commercial expectations into clear actions, following the details through samples and revisions, and giving an overseas client enough evidence to make a sound decision.

That is the work I focus on.

I am especially comfortable with custom products, low-volume requirements, projects that need repeated China-side follow-up, and orders where inspection or pre-shipment evidence matters. My background includes medical-related products and components as well as specialized equipment and cross-industry custom projects.

I aim to be the practical bridge between what a client means and what a supplier must actually do.

How I work

01

Start with the real requirement

A useful brief includes the business goal, technical needs, acceptable trade-offs, quantity, timing and risk—not only a product name.

02

Make progress visible

Clear updates, photographs, videos, comparison notes and open-item tracking help remote teams understand what is happening.

03

Surface risk early

It is better to identify a missing serial number, unclear capability or unrealistic promise before payment or shipment.

04

Stay close to execution

Custom projects change. Practical support means continuing to follow the revisions, not disappearing after an introduction.
